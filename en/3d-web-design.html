Performance

              

            

            
Most 3D sites are heavy and slow. Models get compressed and loaded in steps.

            
    
              
  • Compressed 3D models
    • 
              
  • Loads in steps
    • 
              
  • Simpler version on phones
    • 
            

            
Tap for details

            

            

              
Why it matters

              
Compressed 3D models means a model that would weigh 40 MB reaches the visitor as a few. Without that step nobody waits for a 3D page.

              
Loads in steps means the page shows up immediately and the 3D arrives right after. The visitor never stares at an empty screen.

              
Simpler version on phones means an older phone gets a lighter scene instead of a stuttering one. Nobody leaves because the page will not run.
