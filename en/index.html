About Me

From machining to code

My path into software didn't start in a classroom, it started on the shop floor. As a CNC machinist and programmer, I worked with Mastercam and SolidWorks to design gears and precision tooling, collaborating closely with engineering teams to turn designs into finished parts. Later, as a construction site foreman, I managed full project documentation, organized crews, and took direct responsibility for hitting deadlines.

That background shapes how I build software today: precision, clear process, and real accountability for the result. I bring the same discipline to every app, website, and automation script, because a plan that only looks good on paper isn't good enough. It has to actually work, on schedule, every time.
